    Evidence foundation¶

    This directory supports an original book, not a derivative reconstruction of another textbook. No source-book repository, chapter prose, or paper PDF is included.

    Files and roles¶

    • sources.json: canonical citation IDs, exact bibliographic identity where verified, inspection depth, supported uses, and explicit boundaries.
    • claims.csv: aggregate claim-to-source map assembled from the source audit and chapter fragments. It is not a claim that every manuscript sentence has been formally proved.
    • claim-fragments/foundation.csv: the foundational source audit, retained separately from the authors' chapter claims.
    • data-feasibility.md: a concrete real-data recommendation, provenance and rights assessment, and the limits of venue acquisition.
    • ../references.md: reader-facing bibliography with stable ## ID anchors.

    How to use evidence¶

    Read verification, supports, and limitations together. The ledger distinguishes:

    1. Relevant full-text sections inspected: the named sections were read; this is not a claim that every proof, experiment, supplement, or later revision was checked.
    2. Primary abstract and metadata inspected: claims must stay within the inspected abstract unless an author independently inspects and records additional text.
    3. Publisher-deposited registry metadata: bibliographic identity was checked against Crossref's publisher-deposited DOI record, sometimes supplemented by an institutional page. This is not a reading of the paper. A metadata-only record is a qualified reading pointer, not substantive empirical evidence.
    4. Official documentation inspected: the named content was read from the operator or data provider. It describes the retrieved document, not a historical version or a permanent operational promise.
    5. Partial official response: access or operative terms remain incomplete; do not upgrade this into verified eligibility or reuse permission.

    The explicit registry fallback matters. Several publisher interfaces denied access or returned only citation controls. Follow-up inspection recovered author manuscripts and primary abstracts for ten initially metadata-only records; three remain qualified bibliographic pointers. Rather than invent full-text verification, the bibliography records that boundary. Sources with only metadata should not be used as the sole support for a detailed theorem, numeric result, or operational recommendation. The claim map favors actually inspected abstracts, relevant article sections, actual documentation, and explicitly independent derivations.

    Citation and claim contract¶

    Use [WZ2006](../references.md#wz2006) from chapters. Keys are stable and case-sensitive in JSON; Markdown anchors are lowercase. Multiple sources in CSV use semicolon-separated IDs. locator identifies the actually inspected abstract, section, or documentation heading rather than fabricating a page or theorem number.

    After reviewing changed fragments, rebuild the aggregate from the trading repository root with python book\scripts\assemble_claims.py; use python book\scripts\assemble_claims.py --check for a read-only consistency check. The assembler rejects malformed records, duplicate claim identifiers, missing limitations, and unknown source keys. It does not decide whether a paper actually supports a statement; that remains a source-reading and editorial task.

    Evidence types distinguish theoretical_abstract, empirical_abstract, method_abstract, full_text_section, official_documentation, and data_provenance. A source paper's proof or benchmark is not a prediction-market profitability result. Derived classroom identities, synthetic demonstrations, and untested hypotheses should be labeled as such rather than dressed up with unrelated citations.

    A paper published in a journal may have an earlier working paper or a later repository upload. Keep the publication year distinct from upload date: the Bailey journal article is 2017; the Gelman journal article is 2008 despite a 2009 arXiv deposit; the Hanson journal issue is 2007 despite later web metadata. Manski's NBER working paper is cited as that version, not silently as a later journal article.

    Follow-up source inspection¶

    The following upgrades were completed on the same audit date. The record of reading depth is deliberately narrower than a claim to have checked every paper in full.

    ID Directly inspected material Editorial boundary
    GNEITING2007 Washington author copy: abstract; Section 1; Section 2.1 definitions; Section 2.3; Sections 3.1-3.2, including categorical examples and representation. Scores are positively oriented in the source; this book uses losses. Its categorical Brier sum is twice the single-YES convention. Strict propriety is an expectation property; normalized skill scores require care.
    KELLY1956 All ten pages, 917-926, of the permission-marked Princeton-hosted reproduction. Independent symbols/noise, reinvestment and stake control matter. The conclusion's comparison is against fixed allocations per received symbol, not every adaptive policy. No finite-horizon or estimated-probability safety guarantee.
    AVELLANEDA2008 NYU author copy: main text, Sections 1-3, pp. 217-223; model, approximate equations 29-30 and simulation comparison. Arithmetic Brownian stock price, exponential utility and Poisson arrival model; simple quotes are approximate. Inventory-focused, not a complete adverse-selection model. Simulation and terminal midpoint marking are not executable historical profits.
    GLOSTEN1985 Milgrom's Stanford-hosted reprint, visually read PDF pp. 1-3: abstract and opening introduction. Informational spread with competitive risk-neutral zero-profit pricing and no binding inventory/cash constraint. A stock-market analogy; formal proofs beyond the inspected introduction were not read.
    WHITE2000 Wisconsin-hosted article: abstract/opening p. 1097; Section 2a-2b through p. 1104. Candidate-family maximum versus benchmark; stationary strong-mixing data, asymptotic regularity and dependent block resampling. No cure for undocumented search, leakage or incorrect execution accounting.
    NICULESCU2005 Cornell author copy, PDF pp. 1-3 and 8: methods, dataset/design context and conclusions. Calibration samples independent of base fitting; model/sample-dependent benefits and harms. Random-sample benchmarks do not replace chronological event-group validation.
    RUMELHART1986 Hinton's Toronto copy, visually read pp. 533-535, including equations 1-9 and the local-minimum discussion. Chain-rule derivatives and gradient updates, not global optimization, historical priority, calibration or market-performance guarantees.
    POLITIS1994 Complete primary publisher abstract; author publication listing. Weakly dependent stationary observations and geometric block lengths; original full proof and a suitable event-data block length were not audited.
    BATES1969 Complete primary publisher abstract in the page's structured data. Airline-passenger point-forecast combination can lower mean-square error; not a universal probability-calibration result.
    KYLE1985 Complete primary Econometric Society abstract in raw HTML. One risk-neutral insider, noise traders and competitive risk-neutral market makers; continuous-limit equilibrium properties are not empirical event-market laws.

    GNEITING2007 now supplies directly inspected core scoring definitions and the categorical Brier/log-score examples, so the two inaccessible historical scoring papers do not leave elementary scoring theory citation-only. No such substitution is mislabeled as a reading of Brier's or Murphy's original article.

    Extraction used the already installed system Python/PyMuPDF, without modifying the companion environment or installing dependencies. PDFs were processed in memory and not saved. A small set of local rendered images was used to inspect scanned pages and removed after inspection. Only original summaries, locators, source URLs and claim boundaries are retained; no paper text or figures are redistributed.

    Audit date and change control¶

    All retrievals recorded here use 2026-09-10. This is the audit's access date, not evidence of a site's past contents. No historical source snapshots, copyrighted full texts, or paper PDFs are redistributed. Mutable official documentation must be rechecked before any implementation relies on it.

    Important factual corrections from direct inspection:

    • Kalshi provides a mock-funds demo and explicitly warns against treating its behavior as live-market behavior.
    • Kalshi history is split by moving cutoffs; its inspected historical-data guide does not establish a complete order-book archive.
    • Polymarket fees are market-specific, and official materials distinguish pUSD collateral from USDC-related fee/funding descriptions.
    • Polymarket resolution can involve disputes, clarifications, and a 50/50 payout.
    • An accessible website is not an unrestricted data license or permission for every reader to trade.
    • NOAA's CO2 source is real observational data but current-vintage, partly historically third-party, and not an issued-forecast or prediction-market dataset.

    The strongest supported implementation path is an offline, reproducible forecast-scoring study on an attributed NOAA-period extract, with its current-vintage limitation prominently retained. Venue collection is a separate, permission-gated read-only path, and no source in this ledger alone proves a profitable trading strategy.
