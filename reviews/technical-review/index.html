    Technical review: companion and evidence infrastructure¶

    Current locations of the reviewed materials¶

    This is a historical record of the independent review before directory relocation, not a new code review or new test run. The reviewed educational Python project now lives in book\companion; its paper-only boundary does not describe separate trading-system code at the repository root. From the trading repository root, run Set-Location book\companion before using the companion's .venv, examples, code tests, or offline NOAA command. See the companion README.

    The manuscript and source audit remain separate: book, research ledger, and whole-manuscript editorial record. Manuscript checks now run from the trading repository root with python -m unittest discover -s book\tests -q. The historical 136-test count below is retained as the result of that review, not as a newly measured relocation result.

    Scope¶

    A fresh, read-only code-review agent inspected the new Python companion diff independently of its implementer. The scope covered the paper ledger, contracts, costs, risk and basket decisions, probability models and evaluation, optional public-GET acquisition, offline examples, the NOAA study, and their relevant tests and environment files. The shared notation and example documentation served as behavioral contracts.

    This was not the whole-manuscript editorial read. The separate editorial phase covers all chapters and their transitions. Neither review is academic peer review, a legal opinion, or certification of profitable trading.

    Findings and resolutions¶

    Finding Resolution Evidence retained
    Paper screening reused original depth after the broker had consumed it. paper_decision now screens a read-only remaining-depth view. Screening, execution, and marking share the broker's consumption calculation. After ten units at 0.40 are consumed, a second twenty-unit screen uses the remaining 0.60 asks: cost 12.00 and modeled profit -1.00 at probability 0.55. Repeated screening leaves the original snapshot and ledger unchanged.
    Basket screening counted modeled fill cost but omitted conservative order reservations. The basket reports required_cash and checks enough funding to submit all legs before execution, using the same fee/reservation helper as the broker. Three units on each complementary leg at 0.3333 with an invented 0.001 unit fee cost 2.02 in aggregate fills but require 2.10 reserved cash. The lower capital is rejected; both orders can be submitted and filled with the higher capital.
    Evaluation blocked reused event IDs but allowed new event IDs from fitted dependence groups. Fitted forecasters retain both training and calibration group IDs, and evaluation rejects overlap with either. Regressions supply a distinct event in each fitted group type and require explicit rejection.
    Truncated chunked HTTP responses escaped the collector's structured failure path. HTTP protocol/read exceptions enter bounded retry handling and preserve completed-page and attempt diagnostics. A real in-memory HTTPResponse with a truncated chunk, retry exhaustion, a successful retry, response closure, and prior-page retention are exercised without network access.

    The reviewer then inspected these focused fixes and their regressions, marked all four findings resolved, and reported no fix-introduced high-confidence issues. The reviewer independently ran the 136 companion and NOAA tests present at that point. Later manuscript-integrity checks are a separate activity rather than part of that historical count.

    Frozen-byte portability¶

    Integration also exposed a persistence defect before the independent review: the frozen implementation and protocol included CRLF bytes, but the initially proposed LF checkout rules would have changed their hashes. Passing the study in the original worktree would not have proved reproduction after a fresh checkout.

    The fix preserves the original hash-bound bytes with scoped Git -text rules. No source observations, protocol contents, shared scoring implementation, predictions, scores, or frozen evaluation were changed or selected again. A regression compares Git's filtered and unfiltered object hashes for every bound input and result file. Ordinary unbound package sources can still use their normal LF policy.

    Evidence and operating boundaries¶

    The research ledger distinguishes inspected sections, inspected abstracts, metadata-only reading pointers, and official documentation. At source-audit completion it held 55 sources and a 73-row foundational claim map; authored chapter fragments extend that map. BRIER1950, MURPHY1973, and FRIEDMAN2001 retain disclosed access limitations rather than fabricated full-text verification.

    All seventeen default demonstrations remain offline and explicitly synthetic. The optional acquisition interface preserves venue-native public snapshots, requires deliberate acknowledgement, and has no credential, signing, deposit, funded-order, or automatic promotion route. Its tests use synthetic HTTP responses; they do not establish current account eligibility or redistribution permission.

    The real NOAA study remains a frozen current/final-vintage forecasting exercise with a hypothetical information clock, not historical prediction-market or executable-profit evidence. The paper ledger remains in-memory, single-process, and subject to its explicitly invented USD fee and quantity conventions. Supplied maker fills, complete payout-state declarations, and evidence flags are assumptions to audit, not mechanisms that turn uncertain inputs into facts.

    Review limitations¶

    The review and regressions reduce specific implementation risks; they do not prove the absence of all defects. They do not validate a real exchange's fills, queue dynamics, latency, fees, legal availability, or a strategy's future returns. The accompanying source ledger and data-provenance records retain their narrower, explicitly stated inspection and measurement limits.
