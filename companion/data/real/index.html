    NOAA observational extract: attribution and provenance¶

    These are real observational records, not synthetic examples or prediction-market data. The CSV preserves the source's eight numeric field tokens for January 1975 through December 2025. Only whitespace delimiters and column names were normalized. Flagged rows remain in the CSV so exclusions can be audited; the study does not treat them as observed targets or interpolate replacements.

    Provider and permitted use¶

    Data provided by NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory, Boulder, Colorado, USA (gml.noaa.gov), Carbon Cycle and Greenhouse Gases group, 325 Broadway R/GML, Boulder, CO 80305 (group website).

    • Monthly Mauna Loa CO2 source
    • Data documentation and citation guidance
    • NOAA GML use policy
    • Book source records: NOAA_CO2, NOAA_DATA, and NOAA_POLICY.

    The documentation's current reference guidance credits Dr. Xin Lan, NOAA/GML, and Dr. Ralph Keeling, Scripps Institution of Oceanography, for the broader Trends page. This extract specifically acknowledges NOAA-period measurements; the book authors are not the measurement creators.

    The inspected government-data policy permits public use unless specifically annotated otherwise and requires acknowledgement, no implied NOAA endorsement, and no presentation of modified material as official government information. The incorporated NOAA observations are U.S. government data not subject to the book's original-content copyright. NOAA supplies them without warranty. Normalization, derived binary labels, forecasts, and figures are the book authors' transformations, not official NOAA products. NOAA does not endorse this book or its models.

    The source identifies March 1958-April 1974 as Scripps data. Its third-party status must not be erased by generalizing the government-data policy. The conservative January 1975 boundary excludes those observations, as well as May-December 1974. No complete 1958-onward raw response is redistributed.

    Exact normalized CSV schema¶

    Column, in order Meaning
    year Integer observation year
    month Integer calendar month, 1-12
    decimal_date Source decimal year; not a publication/availability timestamp
    average Recorded monthly mean CO2, ppm
    deseasonalized Source adjusted CO2, ppm; preserved for provenance, never used as a predictor
    ndays Integer contributing days; nonpositive is unusable
    sdev Daily standard deviation, ppm; negative is an unavailable/interpolation flag
    uncertainty Monthly-mean uncertainty, ppm; negative is an unavailable/interpolation flag

    All numeric fields must be finite. Dates must be valid, unique, and strictly ordered; the extract must contain every calendar record in the fixed interval. Monthly record completeness does not mean that every row is valid for a target. Missing/nonpositive concentrations and source flags invalidate any event depending on that source month. December 1975 has nonpositive ndays and negative sdev; April 1984 has negative sdev. Flags are checked independently, not only when multiple flag columns agree.

    Availability, location, and scientific limits¶

    This is the current/final vintage at acquisition, not archived first releases. NOAA says quality-control revisions can change the series. Monthly means include center-of-month correction, and some source records are interpolated. These properties survive a chronological split. The retained average is not a raw sensor reading or a verified historical as-of value.

    The source reports alternate Maunakea measurement during December 2022-July 4, 2023. The sensitivity removes full December 2022-July 2023 source months and every target pair depending on them: December 2022-August 2023 target events.

    See experiments/README.md for the predeclared clock, commands, scores, exclusions, and limitations. This dataset does not contain contracts, market prices, fees, order books, fills, or settlement publication times and cannot establish executable trading profit.
